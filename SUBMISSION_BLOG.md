# 🏟️ Building StadiaPulse AI: Solving Crowd Movement & Wait Times at Large Sporting Venues with Google Antigravity

*A Build-in-Public Technical Case Study for PromptWars Virtual — Challenge 1*

---

## 🚀 The Challenge
Large-scale sporting venues (stadiums hosting 30,000+ fans) face severe physical bottlenecks during match days:
1. **Gate Security Congestion**: Long queues at main gates while perimeter gates remain underutilized.
2. **Concourse Bottlenecks & Safety Risks**: High crowd densities causing transit delays and safety concerns.
3. **Concession & Restroom Frustration**: Long waiting lines causing fans to miss live action.
4. **Lack of Real-Time Coordination**: No unified real-time feedback loop between fans and venue operations staff.

**Objective**: Build a real-world, intent-driven application that transforms the physical event experience for attendees through real-time coordination, dynamic crowd heatmaps, and smart routing.

---

## 💡 Our Winning Solution: StadiaPulse AI
We created **StadiaPulse AI** — a real-time venue crowd intelligence & attendee experience platform built using **Google Antigravity** and intent-driven AI software engineering ("Vibe Coding").

StadiaPulse AI delivers a **Combined Dual-View System**:

### 📱 1. Attendee Experience View
- **Digital Ticket QR Wallet**: Scan event passes to receive instant personalized entry gate routing and seat location mapping.
- **AI Gate Routing Assistant**: Dynamic queue predictions across Gates A–F directing fans to the fastest gate (saving ~15+ minutes per attendee).
- **Interactive Stadium Heatmap**: Vector rendering of pitch & stadium sectors displaying live density overlays (Clear, Moderate, Heavy) and seat locator.
- **Express Mobile Concession Ordering**: Order food and beverages directly via FastLane pickup codes (`FL-42`) to bypass counter lines.
- **Live Restroom Tracker**: Real-time stall availability (e.g. *11/18 open*) and line wait times sorted by shortest distance.
- **StadiaBot AI Assistant**: Conversational assistant answering natural venue queries (*"Which gate is fastest?", "Where is the shortest line for cold drinks?"*).
- **Group Radar**: Location sharing and group meeting point coordination inside the venue.

### 🎛️ 2. Venue Command Center (Ops Dashboard)
- **Real-Time Sensor Telemetry Simulator**: Built-in engine simulating live crowd movements, gate queue fluctuations, and food order transitions.
- **Bottleneck Alerts & Staff Dispatch**: Automatic anomaly detection alerting ops managers to dispatch roving crowd marshals or open overflow gates.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite 8
- **Styling**: Light Glassmorphic Dashboard Design System (`Plus Jakarta Sans` typography, elevated white cards, slate header accents)
- **Icons & Effects**: `lucide-react`, `canvas-confetti` celebrations
- **State & Simulation Engine**: React Context API managing live sensor ticks, order states, and view modes.
